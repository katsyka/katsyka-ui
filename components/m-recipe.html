Strawberry galette

55 minTotalEasyLevel4.8312 cooks
Ingredients4 servings

    Method

    1. Whisk flour, sugar and salt. Rub in the cold butter until pea-sized, then add ice water a spoon at a time until it just holds.
    2. Rest the dough in the fridge for 30 minutes. Meanwhile halve the strawberries and toss with sugar and lemon zest.
    3. Roll to a rough 30 cm round, pile fruit in the middle and fold the edges over in pleats.
    4. Brush with egg, sprinkle sugar and bake at 200°C for 35 minutes until deep golden.
