Tokyo's decorated manhole covers began as a public-relations fix in the 1980s, when sewer construction was unpopular with residents.1 Today more than 6,000 designs exist across Japan, and collectors trade cards that show each one.2

The idea spread to other cities, though rarely with the same budget.1 In Europe the closest equivalent is Bremen's cast-iron covers, made since 1906.3
