How a roastery stopped running out of its best seller

Before. Stock lived in a shared spreadsheet that three people edited. Twice in one month, the Ethiopian single origin sold out on the website while 40 kg of it sat unroasted in the back.

What changed. Green-bean stock, roast batches and online orders now update one count. When a coffee drops under five days of sales, the head roaster gets a message with how much to roast.
