Deploy from the terminal
in one command.

shipit builds, uploads and rolls out your app with zero config. Previews per branch, instant rollbacks, logs that stream back to your shell.

$ curl -fsSL shipit.sh/install | sh
~/harbor-web


Boring on purpose.
No dashboard to learn. Everything is a flag, every flag has a sane default.
FlagDefaultWhat it does
--prodoffPromote straight to production
--env.envRead secrets from a file
--regionautoPin to a region, e.g. fra1
--rollbacknoneRestore the previous release in ~2 s
--watchoffRedeploy on every save
