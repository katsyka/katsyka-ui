Lease agreement, Flat 4B

1. Term. This lease runs for 24 months from 1 February 2027. Either party may end it after the first 12 months with two months' written notice.

2. Rent. Monthly rent is €1,450, due on the 1st. Rent may rise once a year by no more than the national consumer price index.

3. Deposit. A deposit of two months' rent (€2,900) is held in a separate account and returned within 30 days of the end of the lease, less documented damage.

4. Pets. One cat or small dog is allowed with written permission. The tenant pays for professional cleaning at the end of the lease.

5. Repairs. The landlord fixes structural problems and appliances supplied with the flat. The tenant handles small repairs under €150.
