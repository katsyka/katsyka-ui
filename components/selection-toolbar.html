The case for slower software

7 min read · select any text

Most tools are built to be fast at the wrong thing. They shave milliseconds off a click and then ask you to make forty clicks a day that you didn't need to make at all.

The best software I use feels slower in the moment and faster over a week. It asks one good question instead of offering twelve options, and it remembers the answer.

Speed is not the absence of waiting. It is the absence of decisions you shouldn't have had to make. Design for that, and the milliseconds take care of themselves.
