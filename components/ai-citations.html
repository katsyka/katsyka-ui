How long do sourdough starters last in the fridge?

A fed sourdough starter keeps for about 1–2 weeks in the fridge without feeding1. After that it develops a grey liquid layer called “hooch”, which you can pour off or stir in2. Even starters left for a month or more usually recover after two or three feedings at room temperature13, though the first loaf may rise more slowly.
