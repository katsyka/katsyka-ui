A field guide to accessible colour

Contrast is the floor

WCAG asks for 4.5:1 for body text. That's a minimum for legibility, not a target for comfort. Long reading needs more.

Test text on every surface it sits on, including hover states and disabled controls.

Colour-blind readers

About one in twelve men has some form of colour vision deficiency. Never use colour as the only signal: add an icon, a label or a pattern.

Dark mode is a second palette

Inverting your light theme produces glowing accents and muddy greys. Lift surfaces for elevation and desaturate accents.

Sunlight and cheap screens

Your palette will be seen outdoors on a phone at 30% brightness. Thin grey text disappears first.

A checklist

Body text over 7:1 where you can. Controls over 3:1. Focus rings visible on every background. Test with a simulator and with a real person.
